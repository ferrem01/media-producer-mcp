# The job sounds

Each matched to the sound the viewcci reel uses for that job: candidates (CC0
recordings from Freesound and ElevenLabs sound-effect takes, five per job)
were scored against the reel's sound on spectral shape (28 bands, 120 Hz-8 kHz,
the reel's 2.67 kHz bed tone left out), loudness envelope and length; the
closest was EQ-matched toward the reel's spectrum (+/-12 dB, smoothed),
time-stretched toward its length, trimmed, and peak-matched to the house set
(-1.5 dBFS). Nothing is copied from the reel's audio.
